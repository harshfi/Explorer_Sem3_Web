How to get selected

  1. Pick one project, not five

    Choose the one that matches the language you already write. Selections go to people the mentors already know from the repo.

  2. Set it up and join the chat

    Build the project locally, introduce yourself in the org's chat channel, and read its contributing guide before asking questions.

  3. Land small pull requests early

    Start with issues tagged "good first issue" or "help wanted". Zulip, Rocket.Chat and Mifos look for merged PRs before they shortlist anyone. ML4SCI and CERN-HSF ask you to finish an evaluation task instead.

  4. Write the proposal around a listed idea

    When the ideas page goes live (usually February), pick an idea, talk to its mentor, and share a draft before the deadline. Include a week-by-week timeline and links to your PRs.

  5. Submit during the application window

    Contributor applications usually open in March and close in early April. Results come out in May.

Selection counts come from the official GSoC archive, via the open dataset at github.com/nishantwrp/gsoc-organizations. Repository activity was checked from each repo's latest commit on 30 Sep 2026. GSoC organizations and their slots change every year. Being selected before does not guarantee an org will take part in 2027.
